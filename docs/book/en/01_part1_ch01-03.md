# Part I  User Manual

## Chapter 1  What PondGate can and cannot do

PondGate is detention-pond routing software that runs in a web browser. For one detention pond under a known inflow hydrograph, it computes how the water level, storage and outflow change over time. It keeps the inputs, method and results of every run as a traceable record.

### 1.1 The method in one sentence

The software solves the level-pool (storage continuity) equation:

```formula
dS/dt = Qin(t) − Qout(H)
```

Here S is the storage in the pond, H is the water level, Qin is the inflow and Qout is the outlet discharge. The equation is solved with the fourth-order Runge–Kutta method at a fixed step (RK4, Δt = 2 s). Chapters 11 to 13 explain the principle and show hand checks.

### 1.2 What it can do

- Inflow–outflow routing of a single detention pond, giving the peak reduction, the maximum water level and the event-added detention volume.
- "Constrained design screening" and "continuous optimization" under a declared allowable outflow and allowable level, to find feasible pond areas and outlet sizes.
- Download of a traceable report with the inputs, results and scope of application.
- Numerical comparison with HEC-HMS and SWMM results.
- After signing in, saving a case as a project and sharing it with colleagues in the same company (Chapter 2).

### 1.3 What it cannot do

- Rainfall–runoff calculation: the user must supply the inflow hydrograph.
- Channel routing, long-term continuous simulation or ponds in series.
- Field calibration: agreement with other models is called "numerical consistency" only.
- Engineering approval: results are not a final design. Engineering responsibility stays with qualified professionals.

### 1.4 The software will not say "pass" for you

When no feasible design exists within the current limits and search bounds, PondGate shows "Blocked". It lists the dominant limits and the directions for revision, and it does not show "pass". This is deliberate: it stops users from getting a result that looks acceptable when the evidence is not enough. Chapter 18 uses an example to show how to read a blocked result.

## Chapter 2  Accounts, companies and projects

You must sign in to use the official PondGate website. Your company administrator creates your account. After signing in, you have all calculation functions, and you can also save a case as a project, continue it later and share it with colleagues in the same company. A teaching or demonstration version without sign-in has the same calculation functions but no project storage.

### 2.1 Four roles

| Role | Can do | Cannot do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| System administrator | Add companies and accounts for each company | Open company projects |
| Company administrator | Manage accounts of their own company and view all company projects | Manage other companies |
| Engineer | Create and save their own projects and share them with colleagues | Change colleagues' projects |
| Viewer | Open projects that colleagues have shared | Save |

The system administrator cannot open company projects on purpose. This keeps the engineering data of each company inside that company.

### 2.2 First sign-in

1. Press "Sign in" at the top right of the screen.
2. Enter the user name and initial password that your administrator gave you.
3. The system asks you to change to your own password at once. The new password needs at least 8 characters and must be entered twice.
4. If you forget your password, ask your company administrator to reset it. The system does not send passwords.

### 2.3 Order for creating accounts

1. The system administrator first adds the company.
2. The system administrator creates one "company administrator" account for that company.
3. The company administrator signs in and creates the engineer and viewer accounts.

When creating an account, you can press "Random password" and then give the password to your colleague in private.

### 2.4 Save and open projects

- Engineers and company administrators can press "Save / open projects" to save all current inputs as a new project, with a name and a note.
- If you tick "Share with my organisation (read-only for others)", colleagues can open the project but can only view it, not change it.
- The current project name is shown at the top of the screen. When "● unsaved changes" appears, remember to press "Save".
- After you open a project, results do not appear automatically. Go to Module 4 and press "Run routing and evaluation" to compute again. This makes sure that the results always come from the current software version.

### 2.5 Recycle bin and activity log

- A deleted project only moves to the recycle bin; it is not really removed. Press "Restore" in the recycle bin to get it back.
- A company administrator can see the latest 300 actions under "Accounts", for example who created, shared or deleted which project and when.

### 2.6 Where the data are stored

All calculations run inside your browser, and no data are sent during a calculation. Only when you press "Save" are the input values of the project stored in the PondGate cloud database (Cloudflare). Passwords are kept only as encrypted hashes, and nobody (not even the system administrator) can see your password.

Figure 3-2 shows the "Start here" page. The tabs at the top are, in order: Start here, Guided lessons, User manual, Full step-by-step guide, Book (this book), Tutorial videos, Glossary and AI assistant.

- The "Next step" box changes as you work and tells you what to do now.

### 3.2 Tips

- Each time you press a button, a message at the bottom right explains what just happened.
- Switching the language does not change the input values. If the inputs changed after the last run, switching the language reruns the calculation with the current inputs, and the screen warns you first.
- The software also works on a phone. The tab bar then moves to the top of the screen.
